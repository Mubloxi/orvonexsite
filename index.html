Civilization Event May 8-11, 2026

4 Island 100 Player Civilization Event

May 8-11, 2026 5:00 PM - 10:00 PM each day 4 teams, 4 islands, 100 players

Four teams spawn on four separate islands, each fighting to build the strongest civilization and claim the title of Top Island.

Blue Red Yellow Green

Each island has a Core, your team's life source. Defend it, protect it, and keep your civilization alive.

Players automatically respawn unless their Core is destroyed. Once that happens, every death matters.

Build, defend, raid, and outsmart the other islands in a full team strategy battle.

Recording the event is appreciated. Send POVs through Discord after the sessions.
