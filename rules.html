Follow the rules

Ignorance of the rules is no excuse!

These rules keep events fair, watchable, and fun for everyone. If staff explains a rule during an event, follow that call even if it is not written here yet.

  1. Keep chat readable. Do not spam, flood caps, advertise, or bait arguments.
  2. Do not random kill. Combat needs to make sense for the event, the teams, and the staff instructions.
  3. No hacked clients, X-ray, autoclickers, reach, aim assist, macros, auto-place, or unfair mods.
  4. Quality-of-life mods are only allowed when they do not give combat, movement, cave, entity, inventory, or map advantages.
  5. Do not abuse bugs, glitches, dupes, lag machines, border issues, or unintended event mechanics.
  6. No staff impersonation. Staff decisions are final during the event, and arguing can be handled after things are over.
  7. Keep voice chat clean. No screaming, soundboards, ear-rape audio, or talking over staff announcements.
  8. No harassment, hate speech, slurs, threats, or discrimination. This includes Discord, in-game chat, signs, books, names, and skins.
  9. Do not share personal information, private messages, or anything that could be used to target another player.
  10. No ban evasion, alt abuse, VPN abuse, or joining on another account to dodge punishment.
  11. No stream sniping, ghosting, outside callouts, or sharing hidden information with players who should not have it.
  12. Do not grief builds, steal items, trap players, or destroy progress unless the event rules clearly allow it.
  13. Keep Minecraft usernames, skins, and team names appropriate for a public event.
  14. If you find a problem, report it to staff instead of using it. Honest reports help the event stay alive.

Breaking these rules can lead to a warning, kick, removal from the event, or a ban depending on how serious it is.

Zero Tolerance Policy

Cheating, doxxing, hate speech, serious threats, and major event sabotage can get you removed from both the Minecraft server and Discord without a second chance.

RDM (Random Deathmatch)

RDM is a bannable offense. You cannot kill people just because you feel like it. If staff asks why you attacked someone, you need a real event reason.

Some moments will include fights, but this is not a free-for-all PvP server. The goal is a good event with clear stories, fair tension, and enough room for the community to actually play.

Staff Calls

Staff may pause, rewind, move players, or change a rule if the event needs it. Stay calm, listen, and open a ticket afterward if you think something was handled wrong.
